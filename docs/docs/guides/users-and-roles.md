# Users and roles

**Admins** manage users and sites and open every site. Other users only open the sites they are a member of, with one role per site:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| `viewer` | Read entries, drafts and media. |
| `editor` | Also edit entries, save and discard drafts, upload media. On a site in `direct` publishing, also publish, schedule and delete entries. |
| `publisher` | Publish, schedule and delete entries on every site they are a member of, and delete media. |

A site in `pr` (review) publishing only lets publishers put content live; editors save drafts for them.

## Adding members

Site settings > Members > **Add a member**: enter an email and a role. An existing user is added right away. An unknown email asks for the first and last name, then creates the account with a **temporary password**, shown once to copy.

## Temporary passwords

A user created, or reset, with a temporary password must choose their own at the next login: until then, Sumigo shows nothing else. Users change their name and password in **My account**.
